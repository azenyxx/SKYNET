## Qualification boundary

All five stages are **CLOSED — PARTIAL**, not production-ready or fully qualified. Reported demonstrations were primarily limited to controlled offline and synthetic scenarios. Remaining work includes stronger evidence trust and revocation handling, independent generalization evaluation, cross-component consistency and recovery, and broader performance qualification. No claim of AGI/ASI or independently verified scientific discovery is made.

## Next roadmap stage

**COG-015 — Recursive Self-Improvement** is the next planned stage. Its initial scope is bounded proposals, isolated evaluation, independent checks and rollback of experimental state — **not** autonomous modification or deployment of executable code.
