# SKYNET V2 — Advanced Cognitive Architecture

SKYNET V2 is the second major generation of the SKYNET program developed by Cyberdyne Systems.

It extends the V1 foundation toward advanced cognitive architecture, adaptive planning, learning, method development and controlled autonomous operation.

## Engineering Objectives

- Advanced cognitive architecture and execution contracts
- Adaptive constrained planning
- Learning and method improvement
- Persistent knowledge and evidence management
- Deterministic execution and recovery
- Trusted execution boundaries
- Security, governance and reproducibility
- Independent evaluation and qualification

## Development Approach

V2 introduced a structured architecture-first development process with frozen contracts, bounded implementation milestones, security controls and evidence-based acceptance.

Its development established architectural and governance foundations used by later SKYNET generations.

## Trust and Verification

Development execution, trusted operation and independent qualification are distinct states.

Capabilities are not considered independently verified solely because their components or tests exist.

## Relationship to V3

SKYNET V3 builds upon the V2 architecture through Intelligence Engines, advanced learning, novel method creation, knowledge transfer and verifiable improvement.
