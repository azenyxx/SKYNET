# SKYNET Research Roadmap

## v1.0 — Research foundation

64 numbered stages across COG, AGT, LRN, RSI, SYS and VAL; Python code, tests, audited runtime and validation infrastructure.

## v1.1 — Native Cognitive Integration

Connect existing cognitive, agent, learning and improvement implementations to the active SYS runtime through testable adapters and explicit evidence.

## Next research priorities

Goal-directed planning, feedback-driven adaptation, bounded autonomy, metacognitive evaluation, safe improvement workflows and independent capability assessment. Publication of future development snapshots will be selective and evidence-led.
