# Research hypothesis

SKYNET investigates whether a system combining bounded cognition, persistent evidence, independent evaluation and controlled model/tool orchestration can support progress toward ASI-oriented capabilities.

The scope of inquiry includes general reasoning, metacognition, persistent world representations, continual learning, scientific discovery, cross-domain transfer, capability improvement, adversarial self-verification, calibrated uncertainty, parallel cognition and heterogeneous model/tool orchestration. These are research objectives, not a list of demonstrated features.

Capability improvement is distinct from authority to remove external constraints. A system's reasoning quality does not authorize it to change its own permissions, verification outcomes or resource limits.

## Evidence needed

Meaningful capability claims would require independently assessed experiments, clearly defined human baselines, reproducible methods, uncertainty reporting, contamination controls and results that hold beyond selected demonstrations. Architectural completeness, passing unit tests and persuasive model outputs do not establish superior intelligence.

A research hypothesis may be unsupported or falsified. Negative findings, bounded applicability and unresolved evidence are part of the research record. This candidate reports no independently established AGI/ASI achievement or measured superiority over human baselines.
